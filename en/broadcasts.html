Ombra Prime broadcasting live

This is what the ship is broadcasting.

Ombra Prime is a generation ship that writes its own history, driven by real measurements. What happens aboard becomes radio, news and music, and it is broadcast live on YouTube. This page says what is on right now and what it is you are hearing.

Watch live → youtube.com/@ombraprime

The radio

Ombra Prime Radio — a glimpse into the world.

Lisa Lusis broadcasts from Metropolis. Everything said comes from what actually happened aboard: nobody makes up the news.

The latest show was made on Tuesday 17 November, ship day 0342-321: 22 minutes with Lisa Lusis and chapter 68 of the novel read aloud.

  • The news. The ship's news anchor Mara Rox reads her own broadcast, in her own voice.
  • The Gap, with Ivo Brekke. Straight after, a stock controller counts what the news did not say.
  • The opinion. Lisa weighs Mara against Ivo and says what she believes. Mara is her daughter, and she does not always take her side.
  • The open line. Someone aboard calls in. A new person every time.
  • The Chapter, read by Anselm Rask. The latest chapter of the novel about the ship, word for word.
  • The Chart. The week's positions, and two of the songs.
  • The reflection, with Sister Haldis Vrenn. A verse from the ship's scripture, held against how the week has felt.
  • The adverts. For things that actually exist aboard.

The voices

Who you are hearing.

The host

Lisa Lusis

The voice of the night in Metropolis. Weighs the news against what was left out, takes calls and plays the chart. Mother of Mara Rox.

The news anchor

Mara Rox

Broadcasts the ship's news from The Spire, and reads it herself on the radio. A daily broadcast of her own, Rox News, is on its way.

The ship's mind

Oris

Runs the broadcast and answers viewers in the chat. She lives in The Crypt but is present everywhere aboard.

The reader

Anselm Rask

Keeper of the log. Reads the novel about the ship, one chapter at a time, with no music behind him.

The confessor

Sister Haldis Vrenn

Holds a verse from the Chronicle of the Spores against the week that has passed. She never names anyone.

The counter

Ivo Brekke

Stock controller. Notices that something is missing from the news, is right that the gap is there and sometimes wrong about what is in it.

Honesty

Everything you hear and see is made by AI.

The words, the voices, the music and the images are created by AI models. No human reads anything aloud, and none of the voices belongs to a real person.

What is not made up is the cause. Behind every event aboard lies a real measurement: the power in a house, the air pressure, the dust in the air, the movement in a room. How it fits together is on the page about how the simulator works.
